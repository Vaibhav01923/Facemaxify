## What makes a defined jawline?

A **defined jawline** is one where the lower edge of the jaw is clearly visible, running in a clean line from below the ear to the chin, with a visible angle at the back of the jaw and a chin that sits forward. It's one of the features most strongly associated with attractiveness — especially in men's faces, where a strong jaw reads as masculine, and in looksmaxxing, where the jawline is arguably the most discussed feature of all.

How defined your jawline looks depends on two things: **bone structure** (how wide and angular your jaw is, and how far forward your chin projects) and **soft tissue** (how much fat and skin sits over it). This free **jawline score** tool measures the structural side from a front-facing photo and gives you a score out of 100.

## How the jawline score works

The tool runs Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh in your browser (your photo isn't uploaded) and places landmarks on your jaw angles, cheekbones and chin. It measures two things:

| Component | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Jaw-to-cheekbone ratio | 60% | The width between your jaw angles ÷ the width across your cheekbones |
| Chin angle | 40% | The angle formed at your chin by lines running to each jaw angle |

**Jaw-to-cheekbone ratio.** This is your face's V-taper. The target is about **0.78** — a jaw roughly three-quarters as wide as the cheekbones. That gives a face both structure (a jaw that's clearly present) and taper (cheekbones that are the widest point). Men's jaws tend to be a little wider relative to their cheekbones and women's a little narrower, so 0.78 is a middle-ground target.

**Chin angle.** Looking at the face from the front, lines from each jaw angle meet at the chin. A narrower angle means the jaw tapers more distinctly to the chin; a wider angle means a rounder, broader lower face.

| Score | Result |
|---|---|
| 85–100 | Defined jawline |
| 68–84 | Good definition |
| 50–67 | Average definition |
| Below 50 | Soft jawline |

Your results show your jaw-to-cheekbone ratio and chin angle as well as the score.

## Jawline types

- **Square jaw** — a wide jaw with prominent angles, close in width to the cheekbones. Strong and masculine; can look heavy if very wide.
- **V-shaped jaw** — a jaw that tapers from wide cheekbones to a narrower chin. Often considered ideal in women's faces and common in models of both sexes.
- **Round or soft jaw** — the jaw angles and lower border are less visible, often because of soft tissue rather than bone.
- **Recessed jaw or chin** — the chin sits back relative to the face. This is best seen from the side, so a front-facing tool only partly captures it. The [chin ratio analyzer](/tools/chin-ratio) looks at chin size from the front.

## What about the gonial angle?

The **gonial angle** is the angle at the back corner of your jaw, between the vertical part of the jaw (the ramus) and the horizontal part (the jaw body). It's measured from the **side profile**, usually on an X-ray, and is commonly reported as roughly 120–130 degrees in adults. A lower gonial angle gives a sharper, more angular jaw; a higher one gives a steeper, softer jawline.

This tool works from a front-facing photo, so it doesn't measure your gonial angle. The jaw-to-cheekbone ratio and chin angle it does measure describe how defined your jaw looks head-on — which is what most people see in photos and in person.

## How to improve your jawline

The structure is mostly genetic, but how defined your jawline looks can change a lot:

1. **Lower your body fat.** This is by far the most effective change. Fat under the chin and along the jaw is what hides most jawlines.
2. **Fix your posture.** A forward-head posture softens the jaw and creates a double chin in photos. Standing tall with your head back over your shoulders sharpens the jawline instantly.
3. **Grow or shape facial hair.** A beard trimmed with a clean neckline can add width and definition to a soft or narrow jaw.
4. **Take better photos.** Slightly pushing your face forward and down — the "turtle" trick photographers use — tightens the jawline in pictures.

**What has weak or no evidence:** mewing, jawline exercisers and chewing very hard gum. Building up the chewing muscles can widen the lower face slightly, but overdoing it risks jaw pain and TMJ problems, and there's no evidence it reshapes adult bone.

**Medical options** include jaw and chin fillers, chin and jaw implants, and, for slimming a wide lower face, botulinum toxin injections into the chewing muscles. These should only be done by a board-certified specialist.

## Tips for an accurate jawline score

1. **Keep your head level.** Tilting your chin up stretches the jawline and inflates the score; tilting it down creates a double chin.
2. **Pull your hair back** from the sides of your face so your jaw angles are visible.
3. **Relax your jaw** — don't clench or smile.
4. **Use the rear camera from arm's length or further.** Wide-angle close-ups widen the centre of the face relative to the jaw.
5. **Use front-facing light.** Strong overhead light can create shadows that look like jaw definition, which the landmarks don't measure.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know if I have a good jawline?

Upload a straight-on photo to this tool. It measures your jaw-to-cheekbone ratio and chin angle and gives you a score out of 100. A score of 68 or above means good definition, and 85 or above is a defined jawline.

### What is the ideal jaw-to-cheekbone ratio?

This tool targets about 0.78 — a jaw roughly three-quarters as wide as the cheekbones. Men's jaws tend to be slightly wider than this and women's slightly narrower.

### What is the gonial angle?

The gonial angle is the angle at the back corner of the jaw, measured from the side. It's commonly reported as roughly 120–130 degrees in adults. A lower angle gives a sharper, more angular jaw.

### How can I get a more defined jawline?

Lowering your body fat is the most effective change, followed by better posture and a well-shaped beard. Exercises and mewing don't have good evidence for changing adult jaw structure.

### Does mewing give you a jawline?

There's no good scientific evidence that mewing changes the shape of an adult's jaw. Any change people notice is more likely to come from posture or weight loss.

### Does chewing gum improve your jawline?

Chewing can build the chewing muscles slightly, but there's little evidence it improves jaw definition, and chewing very hard gum for long periods can cause jaw pain and TMJ problems.

### Is the jawline score free?

Yes. It's free, needs no signup, and your photo is analysed in your browser.
