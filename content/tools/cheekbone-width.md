## What makes cheekbones prominent?

Prominent cheekbones give the face structure. When the cheekbones are the widest part of the face and the face narrows below them toward the jaw, the result is a sculpted, defined look — often described as "high cheekbones" or a strong **V-taper**. It's a feature associated with attractiveness in both men and women, and one of the reasons models' faces often look so angular in photos.

This free **cheekbone width analyzer** measures how wide your cheekbones are compared with your jaw and your face height, and scores how much definition they give your face.

## How the cheekbone analyzer works

The analyzer runs Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh in your browser — your photo isn't uploaded — and places landmarks at the widest point of each cheekbone, at your jaw angles, and at the top and bottom of your face. It measures two ratios:

| Ratio | Formula | Target | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cheek-to-jaw taper | Cheekbone width ÷ jaw width | About 1.2 | 60% |
| Cheek-to-height | Cheekbone width ÷ face height | About 0.75 | 40% |

**Cheek-to-jaw taper** is the most important: a ratio around 1.2 means your cheekbones are about 20% wider than your jaw, which gives the face a clear taper from the cheekbones down. A ratio near 1.0 means a jaw almost as wide as the cheekbones — a square face — and a much higher ratio means a narrow lower face.

**Cheek-to-height** checks that your cheekbone width is in proportion with your face's length — not so narrow that the face looks long, nor so wide that it looks broad.

| 68–84 | Well-defined |

| Below 50 | Low definition |

## "High cheekbones" — what it actually means

In everyday language, **high cheekbones** means cheekbones that sit high on the face, just under the eyes, and stand out clearly — usually with a slight hollow underneath. This analyzer measures it through width and taper, because from a front-facing photo, a face that's clearly widest at the cheekbones and narrows below them is what reads as "high cheekbones". The vertical position of the cheekbones and the hollow beneath them depend a lot on lighting and soft tissue, which landmarks can't fully capture.

## What affects cheekbone definition?

- **Bone structure** sets how wide and how far forward your cheekbones are.
- **Body fat** is the biggest changeable factor. Fat in the cheeks and along the jaw fills in the taper, while a leaner face shows the cheekbones more clearly.
- **Age.** The fat pads in the midface shift downward with age, which can make cheekbones look less prominent and the lower face heavier.
- **Lighting.** Light from above creates shadows under the cheekbones that make them look much more defined in photos.

## How to make your cheekbones more defined

1. **Get leaner.** Lower body fat reveals cheekbone structure more than anything else.
2. **Use contouring.** Shading just below the cheekbones and highlighting on top is the classic makeup technique for definition.
3. **Shape facial hair.** A beard trimmed shorter at the cheeks and fuller at the jaw changes the face's taper.
4. **Medical options.** Cheek filler can add volume and projection to the cheekbones; buccal fat removal reduces fullness below them but is permanent and can make the face look gaunt with age. Discuss these with a board-certified specialist.

Face exercises and "cheekbone massage" don't change bone structure.

## Tips for an accurate result

1. **Face the camera directly** — turning your head narrows one side.
2. **Pull your hair back** from the sides of your face.
3. **Keep a neutral expression.** Smiling lifts and widens the cheeks.
4. **Take the photo from arm's length or further.**

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know if I have high cheekbones?

From the front, high cheekbones show as a face that's clearly widest at the cheekbones and narrows toward the jaw. This analyzer measures that taper; a score of 85 or above is classed as high cheekbones.

### What is the ideal cheekbone to jaw ratio?

This analyzer targets cheekbones about 1.2 times as wide as the jaw — enough for a clear V-taper without the lower face looking too narrow.

### Can you get higher cheekbones?

You can make them more visible by getting leaner and using contouring makeup. Cheek filler can add volume. Bone structure itself doesn't change without surgery.

### Are high cheekbones attractive?

Generally, yes. Prominent cheekbones are associated with a defined, structured face and are common among models, though overall facial harmony matters more than any single feature.

### Is the cheekbone analyzer free?

Yes. It's free, needs no signup, and runs in your browser.
