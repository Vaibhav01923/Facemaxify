## What is a balanced forehead?

Your forehead makes up the **upper third** of your face. In the classical rule of facial proportions, the forehead — from the hairline to the brows — takes up about a third of the face's height, with the midface and lower face taking up the other two thirds. A forehead much taller than that is called a **high forehead** (or, informally, a "fivehead"); one much shorter is a **low forehead**.

Forehead width matters too. A forehead that's a little narrower than the cheekbones gives the face a natural oval outline; one as wide as or wider than the cheekbones contributes to a heart or square face shape.

This free **forehead ratio calculator** measures your forehead's height and width relative to your face.

## How the forehead calculator works

The calculator runs Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh in your browser (your photo isn't uploaded) and places landmarks on your forehead, temples, brows, cheekbones and chin. It measures:

| Ratio | Formula | Target | Weight |
|---|---|---|---|
| Forehead height | Top of forehead to brows ÷ top of forehead to chin | About 0.33 | 50% |
| Forehead width | Width between the temples ÷ width at the cheekbones | About 0.85 | 50% |

Your forehead type is based on the height ratio:

| Height ratio | Forehead type |
|---|---|
| Above 0.40 | High forehead |
| 0.28–0.40 | Balanced forehead |
| Below 0.28 | Low forehead |

**One thing to know:** your hairline is often hidden under hair and varies a lot, so the calculator measures from the top of the forehead area the model detects, which usually sits a little below the hairline. That makes the measured forehead slightly shorter than a tape measure from your hairline would — keep it in mind if your result is borderline low.

## High forehead vs low forehead

**A high forehead** is very common and often associated with an open, intelligent look — many models have one. A high forehead can also come from a receding hairline, which naturally makes the forehead taller over time.

**A low forehead** puts the hairline close to the brows. It can make the face look compact and youthful, but a very low forehead can make the upper face look crowded.

## Hairstyles for a big forehead

The easiest way to balance a high forehead is with your hair:

- **Fringes and bangs** — straight, curtain or side-swept — cover part of the forehead and shorten the upper third.
- **Side parts** break up the width of a broad forehead.
- **Volume at the sides** balances a tall forehead with width.
- **For men**, textured crops and fringes work better than slicked-back styles, which show the full forehead.

If your forehead is low, slicked-back styles, height on top and a clear forehead make it look taller.

## Can you change your forehead size?

- **Hairstyle** is the simplest and most effective option.
- **Hairline lowering surgery** brings a high hairline down by a few centimetres. It's a major procedure for specific cases.
- **Hair transplants** can restore or lower a hairline, particularly for a receding hairline.
- **Brow position** changes how much forehead is visible — the [brow position analyzer](/tools/brow-position) measures how high your brows sit.

## Tips for an accurate forehead measurement

1. **Pull all your hair back**, including any fringe.
2. **Keep your head level** — tilting it back makes your forehead look taller.
3. **Relax your brows** — raising them changes the height ratio.
4. **Take the photo from arm's length or further.**

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I know if I have a big forehead?

If your forehead takes up noticeably more than a third of your face's height, it's on the high side. This calculator classes a height ratio above 0.40 as a high forehead.

### What is the ideal forehead size?

Classically, the forehead should take up about a third of the face's height, and be a little narrower than the cheekbones. This calculator targets a height ratio of about 0.33 and a width ratio of about 0.85.

### Is a big forehead attractive?

A high forehead is common and often considered attractive — many models have one. Overall facial balance matters more than forehead height on its own.

### What hairstyles suit a big forehead?

Fringes, curtain bangs, side parts and volume at the sides all balance a high forehead. For men, textured crops and fringes work well.

### Is the forehead ratio calculator free?

Yes. It's free, needs no signup, and runs in your browser.
