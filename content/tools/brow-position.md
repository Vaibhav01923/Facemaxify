## Why brow position matters

Your eyebrows frame your eyes, and **how high they sit above your eyes** changes your whole expression. Low brows give an intense, focused look; high brows give an open, alert or surprised one. Because the brows move with every expression, their resting position also influences how people read your mood — even when you're neutral.

Brow position is one of the clearest differences between typical male and female faces: men's brows tend to sit lower and straighter, close to the eyes, while women's tend to sit higher with more of an arch. That's why low, straight brows are part of the looksmaxxing "hunter eyes" look.

This free **brow position analyzer** measures how high your brows sit above your eyes and tells you whether they're low, ideal or high.

## How the brow analyzer works

The analyzer runs Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh in your browser (your photo isn't uploaded) and places landmarks on the peak of each brow and the top of each upper eyelid. For each eye, it measures the vertical gap between brow and eyelid and divides it by the width of that eye, so the result doesn't depend on how big your face is in the photo. It averages both sides and scores the result out of 100, highest at a gap of about **half an eye-width**.

| Brow-to-eye gap | Brow position | How it reads |
|---|---|---|
| Under 0.30 eye-widths | Low brow | Heavy, hooded, very intense |
| 0.30–0.45 | Low-normal brow | Strong, focused expression |
| 0.45–0.60 | Ideal brow position | Open and balanced |
| 0.60–0.75 | High-normal brow | Open, slightly surprised |
| 0.75 and over | High brow | Wide-open, very expressive |

## Brow position in men and women

There's no single ideal for everyone. A **low-normal** position — strong, straight brows close to the eyes — reads as masculine and is typical in men's faces. A position in the **ideal to high-normal** range, with a gentle arch, is typical of women's faces and reads as open and feminine. This analyzer scores the middle of the range highest, so a man with low-set brows can have a very attractive eye area even with a lower score.

## Do eyebrows drop with age?

Yes. Over time, the brows gradually descend as the forehead tissues relax — a process called **brow ptosis**. The outer part of the brow usually drops first, which can make the eyes look tired or hooded. That's why a brow lift is one of the most common facial rejuvenation procedures.

## How to change your brow position

- **Grooming.** Shaping the brows changes their visible height and arch. Removing hairs from underneath lifts the brow line; keeping them full and straight lowers it visually. Over-plucking makes brows look higher and thinner.
- **Makeup.** Filling in the top edge of the brows or extending the tail changes how high and arched they look.
- **Botulinum toxin ("Botox brow lift").** Small injections can lift the outer brow slightly.
- **Surgical brow lift.** For significant brow descent, a brow lift repositions the brows. It should be done by a board-certified surgeon.

## Tips for an accurate brow measurement

1. **Relax your forehead completely.** Even slightly raised brows push you into the "high brow" range.
2. **Look straight at the camera** with your head level — tilting your head changes the apparent gap.
3. **Pull your hair away** from your brows.
4. **Remove glasses**, which hide or shadow the brow and eyelid landmarks.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the ideal eyebrow height?

This analyzer scores a gap of about half an eye-width between the brow and the top of the upper eyelid highest. Men's brows typically sit a little lower and women's a little higher.

### Are low eyebrows attractive?

Low, straight brows are generally considered attractive in men's faces and are part of the "hunter eyes" look. Very low brows can make the eyes look hooded.

### Do eyebrows drop with age?

Yes. The brows gradually descend with age as the forehead tissues relax, usually starting at the outer ends.

### How can I make my eyebrows look higher or lower?

Grooming and makeup change their visible position. Removing hairs underneath lifts the brow line; keeping them full and straight lowers it. Botox and surgical brow lifts can raise them physically.
