## What is a philtrum?

The **philtrum** is the vertical groove that runs from the base of your nose to the top of your upper lip. It's bordered by two small ridges, called the philtral columns, and ends at the dip in the middle of your upper lip known as the Cupid's bow. Almost everyone has one — it forms early in development, where the left and right sides of the upper lip join.

Its length has a surprisingly large effect on how the lower face looks. A **short philtrum** keeps the upper lip close to the nose and is widely seen as youthful and attractive. A **long philtrum** puts more distance between nose and mouth, which can make the upper lip look thinner and the lower face longer.

This free **philtrum ratio calculator** measures your philtrum as a proportion of your lower face and tells you whether it's short, ideal, average or long.

## How the philtrum ratio is measured

Philtrum length on its own doesn't mean much — a tall face naturally has a longer philtrum. So the calculator measures it as a **ratio**:

> **Philtrum ratio = philtrum length ÷ lower third height**

Philtrum length runs from the base of your nose to the top of your upper lip, and the lower third runs from the base of your nose to the bottom of your chin. Both are measured with landmarks placed by Google's MediaPipe Face Mesh, which runs in your browser — your photo isn't uploaded.

The score out of 100 peaks at a ratio of about **0.22** — the philtrum taking up a little over a fifth of the lower third — and drops the further you are from it in either direction.

## Long philtrum vs short philtrum

**A short philtrum** keeps the mouth close to the nose. It makes the upper lip look fuller and shows a little of the upper teeth when the lips are relaxed, both of which are associated with youth. It's one reason a short philtrum is so valued in beauty and looksmaxxing circles.

**A long philtrum** stretches the space between nose and mouth. The upper lip can look flatter and thinner, the upper teeth tend to be hidden at rest, and the lower face can look longer. A long philtrum is very common and not a flaw — it's simply one of the features people most often ask about.

**A very short philtrum** can have the opposite effect: the lips may look crowded under the nose, or the upper lip may not fully cover the teeth at rest. That's why this calculator doesn't simply reward "shorter is better".

**What counts as a long philtrum?** On this calculator, a ratio of 0.32 or more — the philtrum taking up about a third of the lower face — is classed as long.

## Does the philtrum get longer with age?

Yes. As we age, the upper lip gradually lengthens and thins as skin loses elasticity and the soft tissue descends. That's why a long philtrum is associated with an older look, and a short, defined philtrum with a younger one. The philtral columns also tend to become flatter over time.

## A flat or smooth philtrum

A **flat philtrum** — one with faint or barely visible ridges — is a normal variation for many people. In combination with a thin upper lip and small eye openings, a smooth philtrum is also one of the facial features doctors look for when assessing fetal alcohol spectrum disorders (FASD). On its own, a flat philtrum doesn't mean anything is wrong. If you have concerns about a child's development, talk to a paediatrician.

## How to shorten a long philtrum

There's no natural way to shorten a long philtrum — exercises and mewing don't change it. What does change it:

- **Lip lift (subnasal lip lift).** A surgical procedure that removes a strip of skin under the nose to shorten the philtrum and show more of the upper lip. It's permanent and leaves a scar hidden at the base of the nose, so it needs a skilled, board-certified surgeon.
- **Lip filler.** Adding volume to the upper lip, especially at the border, can make a long philtrum look shorter, though it doesn't change the actual length.
- **Facial hair.** A moustache can hide the length of the philtrum entirely.

## Tips for an accurate philtrum measurement

1. **Keep your lips closed and relaxed.** An open mouth or a smile moves the upper lip landmark and changes the ratio.
2. **Hold your head level.** Tilting your head back makes the philtrum look shorter; tilting forward makes it look longer.
3. **Avoid close-up selfies**, which distort the nose and mouth.
4. **Shave or trim** if a moustache covers your upper lip.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a philtrum?

The philtrum is the vertical groove between the base of your nose and the top of your upper lip, bordered by two small ridges called philtral columns. It ends at the Cupid's bow in the middle of your upper lip.

### Does everyone have a philtrum?

Almost everyone does — it forms during early development where the two halves of the upper lip join. Its length and how defined its ridges are vary a lot from person to person.

### What is considered a long philtrum?

On this calculator, a philtrum that takes up 32% or more of the lower third of the face — measured from the base of the nose to the chin — is classed as long. The ideal range is about 18–25%.

### Is a short philtrum attractive?

Generally, yes. A short philtrum keeps the upper lip close to the nose and makes it look fuller, which is associated with youth. A very short philtrum can crowd the lips, though, so moderate is best.

### Is a long philtrum unattractive?

Not necessarily. It's very common and many attractive people have one. It can make the upper lip look thinner and the lower face longer, but how the whole face is balanced matters more.

### Does the philtrum get longer with age?

Yes. The upper lip slowly lengthens and thins with age as the skin loses elasticity, so the philtrum tends to get longer and flatter over time.

### How can I shorten my philtrum?

Only with a procedure. A subnasal lip lift surgically shortens the philtrum, and lip filler can make it look shorter by adding volume to the upper lip. Exercises and mewing don't change philtrum length.

Yes. It's free, needs no signup, and analyses your photo in your browser.
